Torque documentation

Torque is a Shopify theme for auto parts, accessories and tools stores. Shoppers pick their vehicle once, and every product then tells them if it fits. No apps needed.

Vehicle finder

Year, make and model search powered by product tags and Shopify's native filtering.

My Garage

Shoppers save vehicles and see live "Fits your vehicle" badges everywhere.

Built for parts

Part number search, spec tabs, quick order lists, parts diagrams and delivery estimates.

Get started

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes. Add Torque from the Theme Store, or click Customize on Torque if it is already in your library.
  2. Open Theme settings > Vehicle finder and replace the sample list with the vehicles you sell parts for. See Vehicle finder.
  3. Tag your products with the vehicles they fit. Use the free tag generator to build the tags.
  4. Set your logo, colors and fonts in Theme settings, then build your homepage with the sections described below.
  5. Create your main menu in Content > Menus. Menus with three levels open as a mega menu.
Tip: Create one collection per category (Brakes, Filters, Lighting...) with an automated condition such as Product type is equal to Brakes. The vehicle finder works with any collection.

Vehicle finder

The vehicle list lives in Theme settings > Vehicle finder > Vehicle list. Add one vehicle per line in this format:

Make | Model | Years
Toyota | Camry | 2012-2024
Ford | F-150 | 2015-2024
BMW | 3 Series | 2012-2023

The finder builds its year, make and model dropdowns from this list. When a shopper searches, Torque opens your results collection filtered by the matching fitment tag, for example /collections/all/fit-toyota-camry-2019. This uses Shopify's built-in tag filtering, so it is fast and needs no app.

You can show the finder in three places: the Hero with vehicle finder section, the Vehicle finder strip section, and the My Garage button in the header. Collection pages also show a vehicle bar above the products.

Fitment tags

Each product lists the vehicles it fits as tags. Tags are lowercase and use hyphens instead of spaces:

TagMeaning
fit-toyota-camry-2019Fits a 2019 Toyota Camry. Add one tag per year. This is the tag the finder searches for.
fit-toyota-camryOptional. Lets you link to all Camry parts.
fit-toyotaOptional. Used by the Shop by make section links.
fit-universalFits most vehicles. Shows a "Universal fit" badge.
core-chargeShows the core charge notice on the product page.
newShows the "New" badge on product cards.

The make and model in the tag must match the vehicle list, written in lowercase with spaces and symbols replaced by hyphens. F-150 becomes f-150 and 3 Series becomes 3-series.

Torque groups the year tags into ranges in the Vehicle fitment table on the product page, for example "2015–2018, 2020".

Fitment tag generator

Paste the vehicles a product fits, one per line, then copy the tags into the product's Tags field or into the Tags column of your product CSV.

My Garage and fitment badges

When a shopper searches with the finder, the vehicle is saved to My Garage in their browser. The header button shows the active vehicle, and they can switch or remove vehicles at any time.

  • Product cards show a green "Fits your vehicle" or red "Does not fit" strip.
  • Product pages show the badge in the Fitment check block, with a link to the full fitment table.
  • Collection pages show a vehicle bar with a one-click "Show parts that fit" button.
  • Products without any fit- tags show no badge, so non-vehicle items like tools stay clean.

Part numbers, specs and downloads

Torque reads these product metafields. Create them in Settings > Custom data > Products with the exact namespace and key:

MetafieldTypeUsed for
custom.part_numberSingle line textPart number on cards and the product page. Falls back to the variant SKU.
custom.specsMulti-line textSpecifications tab. One spec per line as Name: Value.
custom.install_guideRich textInstallation tab.
custom.install_videoFile (video)Video in the Installation tab.
custom.datasheetFile"Download datasheet" button in the Specifications tab.

Predictive search matches titles, product types, vendors, SKUs and tags, so shoppers can type a part number like RDL-BP-4410. You can turn SKU and tag matching off in Theme settings > Search.

Theme settings

  • Colors: five color schemes. Scheme 1 is the page background, scheme 3 the dark header and footer, and scheme 4 the accent color.
  • Typography: heading and body fonts from the Shopify font library, size scales and optional uppercase headings.
  • Product cards: image ratio, contain or cover, hover image, vendor, part number, stock, quick add and ratings.
  • Cart: drawer or page, order notes, free shipping progress bar (in your store currency) and an upsell collection.
  • Delivery estimate: dispatch cutoff hour, transit days and weekend handling.
  • Search: predictive search and part number matching.

Mega menu

Choose your menu in the Header section. A top-level item opens as a full-width mega menu when it has three levels (for example Shop parts > Maintenance > Brakes). Add a Mega menu promo block to the header and type the top-level item's title to show a promo card inside that menu.

FAQ

Do I need an app for year, make and model search?

No. Torque uses its vehicle list setting and product tags with Shopify's native filtering. You avoid a monthly app fee.

How many vehicles can I add?

As many as you like. Keep each line in the Make | Model | Years format. Very large catalogs can split vehicles across collections and set a results collection per section.

A vehicle shows "No parts are listed yet". Why?

No product has the matching year tag yet. Check the spelling with the tag generator. For example a 2019 Toyota Camry needs fit-toyota-camry-2019.

Can I use Torque for a store without vehicles, like tools or hardware?

Yes. Turn off Enable vehicle finder in Theme settings. Every other feature, including quick order lists and spec tabs, keeps working.

Does the delivery estimate use fake countdowns?

No. It uses your real dispatch cutoff time and your store's time zone, and shows an honest delivery window.

How do I show reviews?

Install any reviews app that supports app blocks, then add its block to the product page. Star ratings on cards read Shopify's standard rating metafield.

Is Torque translatable?

Yes. All storefront text is in the theme's locale file and can be edited in Online Store > Themes > Edit default theme content or translated with Shopify Translate and Adapt.

Theme support

Ask us about setup, the vehicle finder, fitment tags or anything that is not working. We reply within 2 business days, Monday to Friday.

Before you write: many answers are in the FAQ and the fitment tags guide.

What to include

  • Your store URL (the .myshopify.com address)
  • The page where the problem shows up
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • A link to a screenshot or screen recording if you can (Google Drive, Dropbox or Loom)
  • If you edited theme code or use apps that change the storefront, tell us which

Send a request

You will get an email copy of your request. If the form does not load, open it in a new tab.
